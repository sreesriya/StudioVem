Process

Every project starts with strategy. Understanding your goals, audience, and challenges ensures every design decision has purpose.

01

Discovery

  • Intro conversation
  • Goals & audience
  • Competitor landscape
  • Existing assets audit
You receive Discovery summary, project roadmap, timeline
02

Strategy

  • What are we communicating?
  • Who are we speaking to?
  • What should people feel?
You receive Creative brief, brand direction, site or system architecture
03

Exploration

  • Sketches & moodboards
  • Early concepts
  • Wireframes & references
You receive A handful of directions worth reacting to—not fifty options
04

Design

  • Iterations on the chosen direction
  • Structured feedback rounds
  • Refinement & decision-making
You receive Polished, near-final designs at each checkpoint
05

Delivery

  • Brand guidelines
  • Final assets & source files
  • Developer or print handoff
You receive Figma, AI, and PDF files, plus ready-to-use social assets
06

Growth

  • Ongoing design support
  • New campaigns & launches
  • Seasonal & UX updates
You receive A design partner who sticks around after launch
